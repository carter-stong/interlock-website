About Me

Why I Started Interlock Talent

Hi, I’m Carter Stong, a mechanical engineering student at BYU and the founder of Interlock Talent.

Like a lot of engineering students, I applied to dozens and dozens of internships and heard nothing back. When I talked to classmates, almost everyone was stuck in the same spot: strong skills and hands-on experience, but no direct line to the people making hiring decisions.

Once I started working in industry, I saw the same disconnect from the other side.

At a small electrical engineering firm, I was their first mechanical engineering intern and was handed project lead on a root-cause analysis of product switch failures. Designing the tests, finding the problem, and implementing the fix was valuable for both of us, but finding an intern who could step into that role had taken the firm a lot of time and resources.

Later, in the automation department at a mid-sized company, I saw how much time hiring can take. One manager I worked with told me he spent more than 30 hours of engineering time sorting through hundreds of near-identical applications, running multiple interview rounds, and finalizing offers.

I started Interlock Talent to fix that for both sides. Employers get pre-vetted student candidates based on real project work, for a flat fee instead of a recruiter’s markup. Students get a direct line to hiring managers who look at their projects and hands-on skills, not just a resume.

Whether you’re an engineering lead looking for reliable intern talent or a student looking for your next internship, I’d love to connect.
